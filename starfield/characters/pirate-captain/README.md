# Full Starfield Crimson Pirate Captain Guide (Updated Feb 2026, Shattered Space Compatible)

Live free or die! This build turns you into a ruthless space pirate: raid freighters, board merchant ships, hoard contraband, and lead the Crimson Fleet to glory by betraying the UC. Focus on ship boarding, close-quarters shotguns, and high-mobility raiders. No major 2026 patches alter the CF questline—mostly mod fixes for immersion.

## 1. Character Creation
**RP Flavor**: Scarred face, tattoos, greasy hair, smug grin. Voice: Gravelly male/female for intimidation.

| Component    | Choice                  | Why? |
|--------------|-------------------------|------|
| **Background** | Space Scoundrel        | Starts with Pistol Certification (damage), Piloting (ship boosts), Persuasion (surrender ships/negotiate). Perfect smuggler entry. |
| **Traits**   | Wanted, Taskmaster, Spaced | Wanted: Merc hunts + low-HP damage spike (pirate grit). Taskmaster: Crew auto-repairs your battered ship (raids galore). Spaced: +Health/O2 in space/boarding (90% of playtime). |

## 2. Early Game Progression (Levels 1-10: Bootstrap Piracy)
Skip Constellation niceties—RP as a scoundrel ditching "artifact nonsense" for credits.

1. **Tutorial (One Small Step)**: Loot pirates for Coachman shotgun/Grendel rifle. Sell junk in New Atlantis (NA). Buy Frontier ship (~15k credits).
2. **First Raid (Level 2+)**: Jump to Cheyenne System. Scan civilian/freighter ships (weak: 1-2 bars). Target engines (need Targeting Control Systems R1—challenge: Target subsystems 4x). Disable (EM if avail.), dock, board. Kill/loot crew, steal ship. Sell at NA Trade Authority (TA). Repeat for 50k+ fast.
3. **Get Bounty**: Raid UC ships—bounty triggers. Surrender to UC patrol (space hail). Meet Ikande → **Deep Cover** starts CF infiltration.
4. **Burden of Proof (Optional)**: Collect 20 evidences on The Key (post-Rook). Rewards: Memento Mori pistol.
5. **Gear Up**: Loot spacers for Pirate Swashbuckler suit. Buy Boost Pack at NA.
6. **Economy**: Smuggle to Neon TA (Volii Alpha). Avoid scans with Deception R1 later.

**Milestone**: Own 2-3 stolen Class A/B ships. 100k credits by Level 10.

## 7. Mid/Late Game (Levels 20+: Pirate Empire)
- **Raids**: Settled systems (Porrima). Persuade surrender → Loot contraband → Neon TA.
- **Main Quest**: Betray UC (Deep Dive → CF alliance).
- **Shattered Space**: Optional zealot hunts for loot (no CF conflict).
- **Bounties**: Pay off or kill hunters (Wanted trait fun).
- **NG+**: Skills carry; new universes (e.g., CF already joined). 7 variants.

## 8. Outposts & Economy
- **Hideout**: Airless moon (Kryx). Extract Ti + Fe → Sell/ship to Neon.
- **Smuggling**: Payloads skill → Contraband bays. Deception evades scans.
- **Infinite $**: Raid loops + CF boards.

## 9. Companions & Crew
- **RP Crew**: Spacers (hire bars/Key). No moral companions (ditch Constellation).
- **Affinity**: Likes: Loot/kill/loot. Andreja (stealthy rogue) fits.

## 10. RP Immersion & Tips
- **Journal**: Log hauls ("200k from Eklund convoy").
- **No Fast Travel**: Fly manual.
- **Difficulty**: Hard (raids tougher).
- **Creations/Mods**: CF overhauls (2026: Less Fleet More Heat).
- **Max Fun**: Blackest Sea raids (high-risk loot).
